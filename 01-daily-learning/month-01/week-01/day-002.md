Grammar:
Konjuntiv II for polite requests.


Vocabulary:

Ich habe ein Problem mit ...
Leider funktioniert ... nicht.
Es geht um ...

wäre es möglich, ...?

Ich würde gern eine lösung finden.

 Speaking:
  No written script. You can use the ten expressions above, but don't prepare complete sentences.

Round A: Retelling | 5 minutes

Explain the listening episode in your own words. Aim for 60 to 90 seconds. Don't stop to fix mistakes.

Round B: Role-play | 10 minutes

Imagine you are calling customer service. You ordered headphones online, but they don't work.

You need to:
- Explain what happened.
- Say what you have already tried.
- Ask for a replacement or refund.
- Respond if the employee says they cannot help immediately.
Play both roles yourself. Yes, both. It may feel ridiculous. Do it anyway.

Round C: Spontaneous situations | 10 minutes

Speak for about two minutes per situation:
1. Your bank card has been blocked. You need to contact your bank.
2. You received a parking ticket and believe it was issued incorrectly.
Explain the situation, give relevant details and ask for a solution politely.

Round D: Repeat | 5 minutes

Choose the situation where you struggled most. Repeat it without notes.

Your target is not perfect grammar. Your target is to keep speaking when you don't know the perfect word.
5. Writing | 15 minutes
Write an email of 80 to 100 words to an online shop.

Situation: You bought a product that doesn't work. You contacted customer service last week but have received no response.

Include:
- What you bought and when.
- What the problem is.
- What you have already tried.
- What solution you expect.
- A polite request for a response.
Use at least three of today's Konjunktiv II structures.

Do not use a translator. If you don't know a word, find another way to express the idea.
